⌨️ Finger Placement Guide

Rest your left hand on A–S–D–F and your right hand on J–K–L–;. Keep your index fingers near F and J, use the matching finger for each key, and return to the home row after every keystroke. Keep your wrists relaxed and your eyes on the screen.

Start slowly, keep your eyes on the screen, and build speed with accurate finger placement.
