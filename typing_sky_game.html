🎮 How to Play

1. Choose a duration from 1 to 10 minutes, then press Start Game. On mobile, you can also use the button above the banner. Music plays while the game is running. A new sentence appears after you finish the current one, as long as time remains.

2. Your plane starts on the left side of the banner at medium height. A higher WPM over the last 5 seconds moves the plane forward and upward. At 0 WPM, the plane descends; if you do not start typing within 1.5 seconds, it hits a spike and the game ends. The sky turns red below 10 WPM, yellow from 10 to 15, and green above 15.

3. Earn 1 point for every 5 correct characters. When you are logged in, your game points are added to the leaderboard and your typing speed appears there live. You can play without logging in, but your points will only be saved for this session. WPM controls the plane's forward movement and altitude.

4. Type accurately to improve your speed and accuracy. Your results appear when time runs out.
